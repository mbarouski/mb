\cvsection{Work}

\cventry{Software Engineer Tech Lead at Google}{Jul'23 - now}{Warsaw, Poland}

I started working in the GKE UI area where I initially joined a team
responsible for Anthos cluster lifecycle UI. After 7 months working there,
I became a tech lead of a newly formed team responsible for GKE Upgrades UI.
Since the beginning of 2025, the team started owning features end-to-end.

\cventry{Senior Full Stack Engineer at Montro}{Oct'19 - Jun'23}{Remote}

Montro is a pre-owned luxury watches marketplace. I have been working there
from the beginning till the first purchases through the platform as lead
frontend engineer and senior backend engineer, periodically doing DevOps work,
and continue working there.

As a frontend lead, I take application-level technical decisions, advise
engineers, review pull requests, and mentor newcomers.

As a full stack engineer, I deliver features on both ends, e.g. payments
system, search module, listings scraping system, taxonomy module. Also,
I develop and maintain libraries and tools, e.g. configurator of Google Cloud
load balancer based on data about registered in Consul services, and solve
different types of emerging issues.

Tech stack includes Kotlin/Java + Spring, Golang, Vue + TypeScript,
PostgreSQL, Cassandra, Elasticsearch, Kafka, Google Cloud, Terraform,
SaltStack, Nomad, Consul, Docker, ELK, Prometheus, Grafana, etc.

\cventry{Full Stack Engineer at Senior Node}{Jan'17 - Jul'20}{Minsk, Belarus}

Development of server apps (Java/Kotlin + Spring, Node.js), microservices
(Golang), client apps (JavaScript, TypeScript, Vue, Angular, React), storages
(Neo4j, Cassandra, Redis, MongoDB, MSSQL, PostgreSQL, Hazelcast),
infrastructure (Google Cloud, SaltStack, Terraform, Nomad, Consul, etc.),
mobile apps (Swift). Development of and contribution into private and
open-source libraries and tools.
